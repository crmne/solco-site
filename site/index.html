2Beatgrid

Beatgrids that follow every tempo change.

Solco's local AI hears beats and bars in the audio itself. It locks onto a four-to-the-floor kick, finds the bars in a piano piece, and follows a live drummer through every tempo change, bar by bar. It gets almost every grid right: of the 9,434 tracks in my own library, 848 change tempo, and Solco asked me to check just 16 grids.

Real output, not a mock-up. Solco's analysis of four tracks from a DJ's library, drawn exactly as it comes out: every beat and bar line, and the tempo at each beat underneath.

4Character

Local AI figures out the energy, drive and mood.

Trained on how listeners rate music, it scores every track's energy, drive, mood, groove, vocals and peak from 0 to 100, on your computer. Then search by all of it, together with key, BPM and genre, and find the tracks you forgot you had.

Loudness you can mix by
Every track measured in LUFS, so level jumps show before you mix.
Improves over time
When an AI model gets better, only its readings are redone. Nothing else is touched.

Questions

What DJs ask first.

Do I need rekordbox?

No. Solco writes the USB drive itself, in the rekordbox format Pioneer players read. If you still use rekordbox, Solco can import its library, and rekordbox keeps working as before.

Can I still organise my library my own way?

Yes. Your folders, playlists, cues, grids and tags stay exactly as you set them. Solco adds its readings next to yours and only writes to a file when you say so. Do as much by hand as you like, or none of it.

Which players read the drive?

I have played Solco USBs on the XDJ-RX3, CDJ-2000NXS2, CDJ-2000 and XDJ-1000. Any player that reads the classic rekordbox USB format should work, including the CDJ-3000 and XDJ-XZ. Players that read only the newer OneLibrary format, like the CDJ-3000X and XDJ-AZ, are not supported yet. Tell me what you play on when you sign up.

What comes over from my rekordbox library?

Playlists, folders, hot cues, memory cues, loops, ratings and colours. Beatgrids stay behind, because Solco grids every track itself. Your rekordbox library is only read, never changed.

Is my music uploaded?

No. Every analysis runs on your computer. The optional playlist assistant sends only your request and a count of your genres and styles to an AI provider you choose. No titles, file names or audio, and you can point it at a model on your own machine instead.

What does it cost?

The alpha is free. Each alpha build runs for 30 days, and a new one follows. The price of the full release is not set yet; people on the waitlist hear it first.

Which computers does it run on?

macOS on Apple Silicon, Windows and Linux. Intel Macs are not supported.

What does private alpha mean?

Solco is early. I let DJs in small groups, read every report, and ship updates often. Keep a backup of your music, as you always should.

The name

tracciare il solco

Solco /ˈsol.ko/ is Italian for the groove of a record, and for the furrow ploughed before sowing.

Tracciare il solco means to prepare the way. So the music can grow, and you can play.
