Privacy policy

Controller: PlentyLabs UG (haftungsbeschränkt) & Co. KG, Brunnenstraße 169, 10119 Berlin, Germany. Email: hello@plenty.is.

In short

Our pages set no cookies. We count visits with Plausible, a cookieless analytics tool we host ourselves in the EU, which stores no personal data. Fonts, images, scripts and the waitlist form are all served from this site. We only process personal data when you join the waitlist, and only with your consent. The Solco app is not covered by this policy; it analyses your music on your own computer.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., San Francisco, USA). GitHub processes access logs, including your IP address, the time, the page requested, the referrer and browser details, to deliver the site and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA; GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Analytics

We count visits with Plausible Analytics, running on our own server at Hetzner Online GmbH in Helsinki, Finland. It records the page, referrer, browser, operating system, device type and country. Visits are counted with a daily-rotating hash of your IP address and browser; the IP address itself is not stored, no cookies are set, and nothing is stored on or read from your device. The results are anonymous and not shared. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in privacy-friendly measurement of how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Waitlist

The waitlist is run by getwaitlist.com (Waitlist LLC, 440 N Barranca Ave #2090, Covina, CA 91723, USA). The form is part of this site: nothing is sent to them when you open the page. When you enter your email and continue, your browser fetches the current questions from them, and when you submit, your details go directly to them, along with your IP address. They store your email address, your answers to the optional signup questions, and referral data (the link you came from, your own referral link, your place in line and how many people you referred), along with technical details of the signup. You confirm your address through a link in an email (double opt-in), and you can unsubscribe at any time through the link in every email or by writing to hello@plenty.is. The form sets no cookies.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by submitting the form, which you can withdraw at any time with effect for the future. Your data is kept until you unsubscribe or the waitlist is no longer needed. The data is processed in the USA; where no adequacy decision or other safeguard applies, the transfer rests on your explicit consent (Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR), and US authorities may be able to access it.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, to object, and to withdraw consent. Write to hello@plenty.is. You can also complain to a supervisory authority, such as the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).
