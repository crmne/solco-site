Solco against rekordbox 7: how we measured.

The home page says Solco wrote a USB in 12 minutes where rekordbox took 3½ hours, and that its beat grids are 12% better on beats and 38% better on downbeats. Those two are relative improvements in F-measure, the standard accuracy measure for beat tracking, on the 998 excerpts of the GTZAN set: Solco's score divided by rekordbox's, minus one. This page is how all of these numbers were made, with everything needed to check them.

The USB export

Both apps put the same 493 already-analysed tracks on the same 250 GB SanDisk USB stick (FAT32) from a Mac mini M4. rekordbox 7.2.6. We timed three runs of each and report the median: Solco 12 minutes 18 seconds, rekordbox 3 hours 30 minutes.

Beat grids

The test set is GTZAN with the GTZAN-Rhythm annotations: 998 excerpts of 30 seconds in ten genres, with every beat and downbeat marked by people. It is the only public beat set Solco's beat model never trained on. rekordbox 7.2.6 analysed it in its Auto mode with high-precision grids, using the owner's own settings; Solco analysed it with its own grids and no corrections. Both read the same files.

The grids are scored with the standard mir_eval conventions: F-measure, where 1 is perfect; a beat counts if it is within 70 ms of a marked one; the first 5 seconds are skipped. Overall, Solco scored 0.859 on beats against rekordbox's 0.767 (12% higher) and 0.736 on downbeats against 0.533 (38% higher).

For each genre we report the paired difference with its 95% bootstrap interval (10,000 resamples). "Clearly" below means that interval excludes zero.

How we measured

USB export: both apps put the same 493 already-analysed tracks on the same 250 GB SanDisk USB stick (FAT32) from a Mac mini M4; rekordbox 7.2.6; median of three runs.

Beat grids: the 998 30-second excerpts of GTZAN with the GTZAN-Rhythm annotations, people's marks of every beat and downbeat; the only public beat set Solco's beat model never trained on. rekordbox 7.2.6 in its Auto mode with high-precision grids, Solco with its own grids and no corrections, both given the same files. Scored with the standard mir_eval conventions (a beat counts within 70 ms; the first 5 seconds are skipped). "Clearly" means the 95% bootstrap interval of the paired difference excludes zero.

GTZAN is not a DJ set: it shows the grids hold beyond four-to-the-floor, not that Solco is ahead on any one club genre.

What this does and doesn't show

GTZAN is not a DJ set: it shows the grids hold beyond four-to-the-floor, with live drummers and changing tempo, not that Solco is ahead on any one club genre. On beats, Solco is clearly ahead in five genres, within the margin in four, and level on reggae. On downbeats it is ahead in all ten, clearly in nine.
