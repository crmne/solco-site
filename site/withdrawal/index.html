Right of withdrawal

Withdraw from your order

If you bought Solco as a private person, you can withdraw within 14 days. You don't have to give a reason. We pay back everything you paid, by the same means of payment.

That goes for any purchase, whether or not you have activated the key. If you withdraw from a licence, it is revoked and the key stops working. If you withdraw from a renewal, its 12 months are taken back and your licence keeps working.
