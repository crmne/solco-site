3Solco

What Solco is.

Solco prepares music for DJing on your own computer. It grids every track you add, with downbeats and grids that follow tempo changes, works out its key, loudness, style and character, helps fix its tags and covers, finds duplicates by their sound, and files tracks into playlists and smart playlists. Then it writes the USB drive Pioneer DJ and AlphaTheta players read, with both the Device Library and OneLibrary, or a Traktor collection and M3U8 playlists for other apps.

It runs on a Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel, macOS 13.4 or later), 64-bit Windows and Linux. It's in private beta: free, by invitation from the waitlist, with a personal licence key. The terms list the prices for the full release.

Questions

What DJs ask about comparisons.

Is Solco a DJ app for mixing?

No. Solco prepares your music and writes the USB drive or playlists you play from. It has one deck for previewing tracks, checking grids and setting cues, and you mix on your players or in your DJ app.More in the help centre

Can I keep using the app I have?

Yes. Solco imports playlists, cues, loops, ratings and colours from rekordbox, Traktor and Apple Music without changing their libraries, writes its keys into your files so other apps see them, and exports a Traktor collection and M3U8 playlists. Each comparison page says how the two apps can work together.More in the help centre

What does Solco cost?

The private beta is free, by invitation from the waitlist, with a personal licence key. There is no way to buy Solco on this site yet. The terms list the prices for the full release: Solco €149, or €99 at launch for people it's offered to, and Solco Intro €29 for up to 1,000 tracks and 10 playlists.More in the help centre
