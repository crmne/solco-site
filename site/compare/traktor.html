Solcoand Traktor

Traktor is what you play with. Solco is what you prepare with. They meet in the library, where both analyse tracks and keep playlists. Solco grids, keys and tags each track and hands Traktor a collection with your cues, loops and grids.

I make Solco, so read this with that in mind. Every line about Traktor comes from Native Instruments' own site, manual and support pages, listed under Sources with the date I checked it.

1What each is for

One plays. One prepares.

Traktor
"Professional 4-deck DJ software." Its Track Collection is where you "organize, tag, and prepare your tracks for being played as part of your mix". T1 T3
Solco
Prepares new music for DJing on your computer: beatgrid and downbeats, key, loudness, style and character, tags and covers, duplicates and playlists. Then it writes a Traktor collection, or the USB drive Pioneer CDJ and XDJ players read. No mixing. S1 S11 S9

3Where Solco does more

Grids found for you, and the rest of the prep.

  • Grids that follow tempo changes, by themselves. Solco follows a live drummer or a drifting edit and marks the downbeats, then lists the few grids worth checking. S3 Traktor's analysis sets one constant tempo; extra markers are yours to place. T4 T5 Solco's Traktor export carries one tempo per track, below.
  • Character from the audio, searchable and in smart playlists: energy, mood, vocals, style and more. S4
  • Tags and covers looked up in MusicBrainz and Discogs, each change shown before it's written. S7
  • Duplicates across the whole library, found by sound. S8
  • A gig planner that proposes a folder of playlists from your own tracks. S6
  • A USB for Pioneer players, for gigs without your laptop. S9
  • Linux. S10 Traktor's requirements name macOS and Windows. T1

4Where Traktor does more

Everything on stage.

  • Playing. Decks, stems, effects, DVS, Remix Decks, controllers, MIDI mapping, recording, broadcasting and automix. T1 T8 Solco has one deck for checking tracks. S14
  • Streaming. Beatport in the browser. T1 T3 Solco plays only your own files.
  • Several tempos in one grid, inside Traktor. Traktor keeps several grid markers with their own tempos. T4 Solco's Traktor export writes one tempo per track, the one the track keeps most of the time. S11
  • It's established. Traktor Pro is on version 4.5.1. T9 Solco is in private beta.

5Together

Prepare in Solco, play in Traktor.

Let Solco grid, key and tag your new tracks, and build your playlists there. Export a Traktor collection from Solco's Export page. In Traktor, right-click Track Collection, choose Import another Collection, pick the file and answer Collection. S11

Tracks arrive with Traktor's analysis lock on, so Traktor keeps Solco's tempo, grid and key, with hot cues A to H, memory cues and loops. Two limits: one tempo per track, and Traktor Pro 4 hasn't been tested with Solco's export yet. Make the file on the computer that runs Traktor. S11 S10

Questions

What DJs ask about Solco and Traktor.

Is Solco a Traktor alternative?

For preparing your library, in part. For playing, no: Solco doesn't mix. The two work together: prepare in Solco, play in Traktor.

Can I move from rekordbox to Traktor with Solco?

Solco imports your rekordbox playlists, cues, loops, ratings and colours and exports them as a Traktor collection. It grids every track itself instead of taking rekordbox's grids.More in the help centre

Will Traktor overwrite Solco's grids?

Not while the analysis lock is on, which is how the tracks arrive. Turn it off in Traktor if you want Traktor's own analysis.More in the help centre
