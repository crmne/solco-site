Solcoand rekordbox

rekordbox prepares music and plays it: analysis, playlists and the USB, then decks, samplers and effects. Solco does the first part only: it grids, keys and tags every track and writes the USB your Pioneer players read, without rekordbox.

I make Solco, so read this with that in mind. Every line about rekordbox comes from rekordbox's own site, manual and release notes, listed under Sources with the date I checked it. The two tests are mine, each with its conditions.

rekordbox
"DJ software that enables a comfortable DJ workflow with AI, cloud, and automation tech." It prepares your library, exports USBs for Pioneer DJ and AlphaTheta players, and plays with decks, samplers and controllers. R1 R7 R11
Solco
Prepares new music for DJing on your computer: beatgrid and downbeats, key, loudness, style and character, tags and covers, duplicates and playlists. Then it writes the USB drive Pioneer CDJ and XDJ players read. One deck for checking tracks; no mixing. S1 S9 S14

3Where Solco does more

The USB, and hearing each track.

  • Writing the USB, in our test. The same 493 already-analysed tracks to the same stick: Solco 12 minutes 18 seconds, rekordbox 7.2.6 3 hours 30 minutes. S20
    250 GB SanDisk stick, FAT32, Mac mini M4, median of three runs, October 2026. rekordbox 7.2.20, today's version, hasn't been timed.
  • Beat grids on a public test set. On 998 hand-marked GTZAN excerpts, Solco's grids scored 0.859 on beats and 0.736 on downbeats, rekordbox 7.2.6's 0.767 and 0.533 (F-measure, 1 is perfect). S20
    30-second excerpts in ten genres, not DJ tracks; rekordbox in its Auto mode. Level on reggae beats.
  • Linux. Solco runs on x86-64 and ARM64 Linux. S10 rekordbox's download page lists Windows and macOS. R2
  • Tags and covers looked up. Solco proposes tags and covers from MusicBrainz and Discogs, and writes nothing until you apply it. S7
  • Duplicates by sound. An MP3 and a FLAC of the same recording, under different names. S8 rekordbox lists tracks with the same title and artist. R4
  • Character from the audio, searchable: energy, mood, vocals, style and more. S4
  • No account. A licence key, entered once. S12 rekordbox needs an AlphaTheta account. R2

4Where rekordbox does more

Playing, and everything around it.

  • Playing. Decks, samplers, DVS, stems, streaming, lighting and video. R11 R12 Solco has one deck for checking tracks. S14
  • Automatic cue points. Intelligent Cue Creation on the paid plans. R4 R5 Solco doesn't set cues for you. S14
  • Analysis time, in our test. 4,093 files on the same Mac: rekordbox 72 minutes, Solco 83. S20
  • Small re-exports, in our test. An unchanged 97-track playlist: rekordbox 8.0 seconds, Solco 11.7, because Solco reads every file back to check it. For 493 tracks, Solco 45.9 and rekordbox 72.2. S20
  • Phones, tablets and the cloud. Mobile apps and Cloud Library Sync. R9 R10 Solco has neither.
  • It's established. rekordbox is on version 7.2.20. R3 Solco is in private beta.

5Together

Using both.

Solco only reads your rekordbox 6 or 7 library: it imports playlists, folders, hot cues, memory cues, loops, ratings and colours, and leaves rekordbox as it was. It grids every track itself. S2

One USB can go back and forth. Solco adds to drives rekordbox made and keeps rekordbox's tracks, playlists and played sets; rekordbox keeps Solco's playlists when it exports to the drive, and rekordbox 7 reads the grids, cues and waveforms Solco writes. Quit rekordbox before Solco updates a drive rekordbox has open. S10

Questions

What DJs ask about Solco and rekordbox.

Do I need rekordbox to make a CDJ USB?

No. Solco writes the Device Library and OneLibrary itself, so older and newer Pioneer players list the tracks.More in the help centre

Will Solco change my rekordbox library?

No. It reads your rekordbox 6 or 7 library and never writes to it.More in the help centre

Is Solco a rekordbox alternative?

For preparing your library and writing the USB, yes. For playing, no: Solco doesn't mix, and rekordbox does.

Does rekordbox run on Linux?

rekordbox's download page lists Windows and macOS, as of November 2026. Solco runs on Linux, as DEB and RPM packages or a portable archive.More in the help centre
