Solcoand Engine DJ

Engine DJ and Solco do a similar job for different players. Engine DJ Desktop prepares your music and writes the drive for Denon DJ, Numark and RANE hardware. Solco prepares your music and writes the drive for Pioneer DJ and AlphaTheta players.

I make Solco, so read this with that in mind. Every line about Engine DJ comes from its own site, user guide and help centre, listed under Sources with the date I checked it.

1What each is for

Two prep apps, for different players.

Engine DJ
"A professional DJ collection management software that simplifies music library organization and track preparation workflows." You prepare on the computer and perform on Engine OS hardware. E1 E4
Solco
Prepares new music for DJing on your computer: beatgrid and downbeats, key, loudness, style and character, tags and covers, duplicates and playlists. Then it writes the USB drive Pioneer CDJ and XDJ players read. No mixing. S1 S9 S14

3Where Solco does more

Pioneer drives, and grids found for you.

  • Pioneer drives. Solco writes the drive Pioneer CDJ and XDJ players read. S9 Engine DJ's pages don't describe writing one.
  • Grids that follow tempo changes, by themselves, with downbeats, and a short list worth checking. S3 Engine DJ offers anchors you insert for changing tempo. E4
  • Character from the audio: energy, mood, vocals, style and more. S4
  • Tags and covers looked up, duplicates by sound, and a gig planner. S7 S8 S6
  • Linux. S10 Engine DJ offers Mac and Windows downloads. E3

4Where Engine DJ does more

Engine OS hardware.

  • Engine OS players. It writes the drives for Denon DJ, Numark and RANE players, syncs edits back from them, and lets them load tracks from your computer over the network. E4 Solco does none of this.
  • Imports from Serato. E2 Solco doesn't read Serato libraries. S2
  • Stems and streaming on the hardware. Stems rendered on the computer for the players, and six streaming services on them. E7 E8
  • No price shown. The download page lists Desktop without one. E3

5Together

Two kinds of players.

If you play both Pioneer and Engine OS hardware, one way is to prepare in Solco and write your Pioneer USB from it, and keep Engine DJ for the Engine drive. Solco writes its keys and the tags you accept into the music files, so Engine DJ reads them too. S15 S7

Engine DJ's help says the PRIME 4 G2 "can natively read Rekordbox-exported media drives". E9 A Solco drive hasn't been tried on an Engine OS player.

Questions

What DJs ask about Solco and Engine DJ.

Can Solco write a drive for Denon DJ players?

No. Solco writes the drives Pioneer DJ and AlphaTheta players read. For Engine OS hardware, use Engine DJ.More in the help centre

Can I bring my Serato crates into Solco?

Not directly. Solco imports rekordbox, Traktor and Apple Music.More in the help centre

Do Engine OS players read a Solco USB?

Not tried. Engine DJ says the PRIME 4 G2 reads drives rekordbox exported; a Solco drive hasn't been tested on one.
