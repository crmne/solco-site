Solcoand Serato DJ

Serato DJ is what you play with: a laptop, a controller or turntables, and your crates. Solco is what you prepare with, and its main output is a USB drive for Pioneer players, which Serato doesn't make. They meet in the library, loosely: Solco gives Serato tagged files and playlists, not cues or grids.

I make Solco, so read this with that in mind. Every line about Serato comes from Serato's own site, help centre and announcements, listed under Sources with the date I checked it.

3Where Solco does more

The Pioneer USB, and the prep.

  • A USB for Pioneer players. Solco writes the drive CDJs and XDJs read on their own. S9 Serato connects to CDJs as controllers for the laptop. D1
  • Character from the audio, searchable and in smart playlists: energy, mood, vocals, style and more. S4
  • Tags and covers looked up in MusicBrainz and Discogs, each change shown before it's written. S7
  • Duplicates found by sound. S8
  • A gig planner that proposes a folder of playlists from your own tracks. S6
  • Linux. S10 Serato's requirements list macOS and Windows. D2

4Where Serato DJ does more

Everything on stage.

  • Playing. Decks, real-time stems, five streaming services, certified controllers, DVS, sampler, effects, recording and video. D1 D17 Solco has one deck for checking tracks. S14
  • Tempo tracking inside the app you play. Lucid Beatgrids follow tempo changes in Serato itself. D4 Solco's grids reach Serato only through the files; its playlists carry no grids. S11
  • A free version, Serato DJ Lite, and an app for iPhone and iPad. D13 D18
  • It's established. Serato DJ Pro is on version 5.1.0. D2 Solco is in private beta.

5Together

Tagged files and playlists.

Let Solco tag your files, write its keys into them and fix their covers, then build playlists in Solco and export them as M3U8 files. S15 S11 Serato DJ 5 imports playlist files from other DJ software. D4 Cues and grids don't travel this way, and Serato analyses the tracks itself; it can show the key from the tag instead of its own. D6

I haven't tested Solco's M3U8 files in Serato DJ 5 yet. If your nights are on CDJs, Solco's USB carries the grids and cues to the players directly.

Questions

What DJs ask about Solco and Serato DJ.

Is Solco a Serato alternative?

Not for playing. It's an alternative to preparing a library inside Serato, and a way to make USBs for Pioneer players.

Can Solco import my Serato crates and cues?

No. Solco imports rekordbox, Traktor and Apple Music.More in the help centre

Can I play Solco's USB on CDJs at a Serato gig?

The CDJs read Solco's USB on their own. Serato on a laptop uses its own library.More in the help centre
