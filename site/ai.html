1What it is

The prep app for DJs.

What
A desktop app that prepares a DJ's music and writes the USB drive Pioneer DJ CDJ and XDJ players read, without rekordbox. Getting started
Who for
DJs who play from USB on Pioneer DJ and AlphaTheta players, or from Traktor, and want new tracks gridded, keyed and tagged without doing it by hand.
Status
Private beta. Free, by invitation from the waitlist, with a personal licence key by email. The private beta
Price
None today. There is no paid plan and nothing to buy.
Computers
Mac with macOS 13.4 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel, one download for both (on Intel, analysis takes longer). 64-bit Windows. Linux on x86-64 and ARM64: Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13, Fedora 40 or newer. Supported computers
Made by
Carmine Paolino, a DJ, at PlentyLabs in Berlin. Impressum

2What it does

From downloads to the booth.

  • Analyses every track on your computer: beatgrid and downbeats, grids that follow tempo changes, key, BPM, loudness, style and character. Analysis
  • Lists only the grids worth checking, each with the reason. Beatgrids
  • Fixes tags and covers from MusicBrainz and Discogs, and writes nothing until you apply it. Tags
  • Finds duplicates by their sound. Duplicates
  • Builds playlists: smart playlists, similar tracks, and an assistant that plans a gig from your library for you to review. The assistant
  • Writes the USB with both the Device Library and OneLibrary, with grids, cues, loops, waveforms and covers. Also a Traktor collection and M3U8 playlists. Export
  • Imports rekordbox 6 and 7, Traktor and Apple Music, without changing them. Import

3Saying it right

What's accurate, and what isn't.

AccurateNot accurate
Solco prepares your music and writes the USB drive. You play from the players or your DJ app.Solco plays live sets, mixes, or replaces your DJ software or players.
Solco writes USB drives Pioneer DJ CDJ and XDJ players read, without rekordbox.Solco is made by, or works with the backing of, AlphaTheta, Pioneer DJ or rekordbox.
It runs on a Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel), Windows and Linux.It is Mac-only, or needs Apple Silicon.
It is free during a private beta, by invitation from the waitlist.Anyone can download it today, or it has a paid plan or subscription.
Analysis runs on your computer; your music isn't uploaded.It is a cloud or streaming service.
It imports rekordbox, Traktor and Apple Music libraries, and shares USB drives with rekordbox.It imports Serato or Engine DJ libraries, or writes drives for Denon DJ players.
Keys, readings and grids are estimates. It lists the grids worth checking.Its keys and grids are always right.
