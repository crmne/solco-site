1The plans

Four plans, one of them free.

rekordbox 7 has a Free plan and three paid ones, each billed monthly or yearly, and a Cloud Option that adds 1 TB of Dropbox storage to Free, Core or Creative. Professional includes 5 TB. The Free plan, in rekordbox's words, is "Perfect if you want to start managing your music and try DJing for free."

2Free

What the Free plan includes.

On rekordbox's plan comparison, the Free plan has these, among others. Four of them need owner registration: connecting rekordbox Free once to your own Pioneer DJ equipment, such as a CDJ-3000 or CDJ-2000NXS2.

  • Export mode. Preparing tracks and putting your playlists on a USB drive for CDJs and XDJs. rekordbox's own guide to the Free plan calls it "free use of Export mode (for transferring tracks to USB drives and playing them on CDJs and XDJs)".
  • Performance mode with mouse and keyboard. Mixing on the computer, with 16 hot cue pads and STEMS.
  • Music management. Auto Relocate for moved files, streaming services, the Inflyte promo service, mobile library sync, vocal position on the waveform, two skins and three waveform colours.
  • After owner registration. Collection Filter, Column View, Playlist Search and DUAL PLAYER.

Sources: rekordbox, Plans & pricing, the comparison table's Free column, and rekordbox ver. 7 FAQ, "What is owner registration?", both read on 13 November 2026. Export mode's quote is from rekordbox's guide to the Free plan, published in May 2020 for version 6; the current plan page lists no plan requirement for exporting to USB.

3Paid, or unlocked

What needs a paid plan, or Hardware Unlock.

The plan comparison leaves these out of the Free plan: controlling rekordbox with DJ equipment, DVS, MIDI Learn, recording and sharing mixes, Sample Scratch, Groove Circuit, Mix Point Link, Merge FX and the DJM and RMX effects, video, lighting scenes, saving edited tracks, five screen layouts, Intelligent Cue Creation, cloud analysis, and cloud storage and library sync.

Hardware Unlock
"When you connect a Hardware Unlock device, you can use it to control PERFORMANCE mode without subscribing to a paid plan." With it come recording, sharing mixes, MIDI Learn, Merge FX and Sample Scratch.
Which equipment
rekordbox's list, as of July 2026, includes the CDJ-3000X, CDJ-1500X, CDJ-3000, XDJ-AZ, XDJ-RX3, OPUS-QUAD, OMNIS-DUO and controllers such as the DDJ-FLX4 and DDJ-400.
DVS
Some Hardware Unlock mixers, such as the DJM-S11 and DJM-V10, also unlock DVS.

Sources: rekordbox, Plans & pricing, and rekordbox ver. 7 FAQ, "What are Hardware Unlock devices for rekordbox ver. 7?" (list correct as of July 2026), read on 13 November 2026. The FAQ has the full list.

4Solco

Prep and USB.
That's the job.

Where Solco fits.

If you play from USB, rekordbox Free can already put your playlists on a stick. Solco does that part another way: it grids, keys, tags and sorts every track on your computer, finds duplicates by their sound, and writes the USB Pioneer CDJ and XDJ players read, with no rekordbox needed. It doesn't mix: its deck plays one track at a time, for checking grids and setting cues.

How Solco writes a CDJ USB
  • Free during the private beta. Your personal key comes from the waitlist. The full release's prices are in the terms of sale: Solco €149 with 12 months of updates, €99 at launch, and Solco Intro €29, up to 1,000 tracks and 10 playlists.
  • A licence you keep. No subscription: when the updates end, Solco keeps working.
  • Mac, Windows and Linux. rekordbox's download page offers Windows and macOS; Solco runs on Linux too.
  • Works beside rekordbox. Solco imports your rekordbox playlists and cues, only reading its library, and one USB drive can go back and forth between the two.
  • Where rekordbox does more. Mixing, DJ equipment control, DVS, streaming, stems, recording, video and lighting are rekordbox's, not Solco's.

Questions

What DJs ask about rekordbox Free.

Is rekordbox free?

Yes. rekordbox has a Free plan at €0 and is free to download. The paid plans, Core, Creative and Professional, started at €10 a month billed yearly (€120 a year) on rekordbox's plans page on 13 November 2026, as shown from Germany.

Can I export to USB for CDJs with rekordbox Free?

rekordbox's own guide to the Free plan says Export mode, for putting tracks on USB drives and playing them on CDJs and XDJs, is free to use. The guide dates from 2020; the current plan page lists no plan requirement for it.

What is Hardware Unlock?

Pioneer DJ and AlphaTheta equipment that lets you control rekordbox's Performance mode without a paid plan when it's connected, such as the CDJ-3000X, XDJ-AZ or DDJ-FLX4. rekordbox's ver. 7 FAQ keeps the full list.

Is Solco free?

During the private beta, yes, with a personal licence key from the waitlist. The full release's prices are in the terms of sale: Solco €149, €99 at launch, and Solco Intro €29, each with 12 months of updates, and your copy keeps working after that.More in the help centre

Does Solco replace rekordbox?

For preparing your music and writing the USB your Pioneer players read, it can. It doesn't mix, control DJ equipment or stream. It imports from rekordbox, only reading its library, and shares USB drives with it.More in the help centre
