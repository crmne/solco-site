7. The playlist assistant

The assistant plans playlists from your library when you ask it to. It is included while your updates run, with no credits to buy, up to a monthly number of messages per licence shared across its computers. The app tells you when a month's messages are used up; they come back the next month. It needs an internet connection.

The assistant is an AI system and can get things wrong. Check its playlists before you play them out. Nothing changes in your library until you approve it.

8. Your music and your data

Solco analyses your music on your computer. Your music, your library and its analysis stay there. Activating a key needs the internet once on each computer; after that Solco works offline. What the app sends when you activate a key, check for updates, use the assistant or choose to share something with us is described in the app privacy notice.

Solco keeps backups of its own library. Keep backups of your music files too, as you would anyway.
