1. What you buy

A personal licence to use Solco on up to three computers, with 12 months of updates and the playlist assistant. A renewal adds 12 months. Renewing after updates ended gives 12 months from that day.

2. A licence never stops Solco

After your updates end, the last version released inside your period keeps working. Only updates and the assistant stop.

3. The assistant

The assistant is included for your period, with no credits to buy, at about 100 messages a month per licence. It is an AI and can get things wrong: check its playlists before you play them out.

4. Order and contract

You order with the order button on getsolco.com. Stripe then takes the payment, and the contract is made when it goes through. An order not paid lapses.

6. Right of withdrawal

7. Refunds

A full refund revokes the licence, or for a renewal takes back its 12 months.
