Solco · Cues

Hot cues play.Memory cues wait

Both mark a point in a track, and both go on your USB. A hot cue sits on a pad and plays from its point the moment you hit it. A memory cue sits on the waveform and waits: call it up, and the track is ready to start there. Most DJs use both.

Here is what each one does on Pioneer CDJ and XDJ players, how many a track can carry, and how to set them in Solco so they land on the stick the way you left them.

1The difference

One fires. One marks the spot.

A hot cue is for playing: a drop, a vocal, a break you want to throw in live. A memory cue is for finding your way: the first beat, where you mix in, the breakdown you're aiming for.

On the playerA pad: HOT CUE A to H on a CDJ-3000XA mark on the waveform, reached with CUE/LOOP CALL
Press itPlays from that point straight awayJumps there and waits for you to play

Some older players have fewer hot cue pads; check your player's manual.

2Setting them

Set them in Solco like on a CDJ.

Select a track and it loads on Solco's deck, with its waveform and beatgrid. The deck works like a player: the cues you set there go on your USB drive and into your Traktor exports. With Q (quantize) on, every cue and loop lands on the nearest beat.

  1. Hot cues on the pads. Click an empty pad, A to H, or press Alt+1 to Alt+8 (⌥1 to ⌥8 on a Mac). Click a pad with a cue to jump there.
  2. A memory cue at the playhead. Click Memory, or press M. Alt+← and Alt+→ call the memory cue before or after the playhead.
  3. Loops on either. Start a loop with the loop control, then click a pad to keep it as a hot loop, or Memory to keep it as a memory loop.
  4. Name and colour. Right-click a cue on the waveform and choose Rename cue… to change its name, type, pad, position or colour.
  5. Check it on the deck. A track loads on its first memory cue, as it will on the player, or at the start when it has none.

3On the USB

Set at home.
There on stage.

Every cue goes on the stick.

Solco writes hot cues, memory cues and loops onto the USB drive Pioneer CDJ and XDJ players read, with their names and colours, beside the beatgrids and waveforms. No rekordbox needed.

How the USB export works
  • Pads A to H. Players show eight hot cues. Solco's deck has more pads, I to P, from the waveform's menu: those go on the drive as memory cues, and the export plan names the tracks.
  • Colours the players know. Each hot cue's colour becomes the nearest of the 62 the players have.
  • Cues come back. Cues you set, move or delete on the player come into Solco with your next export to that drive.
  • Nothing lost. If you changed the same hot cue in both places, yours stays on its pad and the player's is kept beside it as a memory cue, such as "Player hot cue A".
  • Bring your old cues. Hot cues, memory cues and loops come over from rekordbox, Traktor and rekordbox USB drives. The other app's library is only read.

4Using both

A simple way to use both.

There is no single right way, but this one works on any player.

Memory cue on the one
The first beat you'd start from, so the track loads ready to go.
Memory cues at the turns
Mix-in, breakdown, last chorus, outro. You see them coming on the waveform.
Hot cues for the moments
The drop, the vocal hook, a loop you like to throw in.
Same pads, every track
Keep the same job on the same pad, and your hands learn it.

Questions

What DJs ask about cues.

What is the difference between a memory cue and a hot cue?

A hot cue sits on a pad and plays from its point the moment you press it. A memory cue is a mark on the track: you call it up with the player's CUE/LOOP CALL buttons, and the track waits there for you to play.More in the help centre

How many hot cues does a CDJ show?

Recent Pioneer players such as the CDJ-3000X have eight hot cue pads, A to H; some older players have fewer. Solco takes sixteen, A to P. Those after H go on the USB as memory cues, and the export plan names the tracks concerned.More in the help centre

Do my rekordbox cues come into Solco?

Yes. Hot cues, memory cues and loops come over with your playlists, ratings and colours, from the rekordbox library or from a rekordbox USB drive. Your rekordbox library is only read, never changed.More in the help centre

Do cues I set on the CDJ come back into Solco?

Yes. The next time you export to that drive, the plan says how many tracks have cues set on the player, and they come into your library first, then go back on the drive with yours.More in the help centre

Can a loop be a hot cue or a memory cue?

Both. With a loop playing on Solco's deck, click a pad to keep it as a hot loop, or Memory to keep it as a memory loop. Both go on your USB.More in the help centre
