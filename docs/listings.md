# Free listings

Ready-to-paste copy for software directories, checked on 26 November 2026.
Nothing here has been submitted and no account was made. Free listings
only: decline anything that needs a paid plan, a backlink or a badge.

Rules for every listing: the same facts as [/ai](https://getsolco.com/ai)
(private beta, free by invitation, no paid plan yet; Mac with Apple Silicon
or Intel, Windows, Linux; prepares music and writes USBs, doesn't mix). No
model or vendor names, no claim against another app beyond what the
comparison pages say, British spelling, no em dashes. Link
`https://getsolco.com` without UTM tags where a site forbids them
(AlternativeTo does); elsewhere use `?utm_source=<site>&utm_medium=listing&utm_campaign=directory`
([analytics.md](analytics.md)), which the form's `origin` then reads as
`other`.

## Competitors to list Solco as an alternative to

For the prep and USB job only, as the comparison pages say:
rekordbox, Lexicon, Mixed In Key, MIXO. Not Traktor, Serato DJ or Engine DJ
(those are for playing, or for other players), and never Mixxx or VirtualDJ.
